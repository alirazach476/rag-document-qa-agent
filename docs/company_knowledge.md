# Company Knowledge Base Sample

## Product Overview
Aether Analytics is a SaaS platform for real-time business intelligence.
Customers can connect SQL databases, CSV uploads, and REST APIs.

## Pricing Plans
- Starter: $49/month — 3 users, 10 dashboards
- Growth: $149/month — 15 users, unlimited dashboards
- Enterprise: custom pricing with SSO and dedicated support

## Support Policy
Support tickets are answered within 24 hours on business days.
Enterprise customers get a dedicated Slack channel and 4-hour SLA.

## Security
Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
We are SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant.
